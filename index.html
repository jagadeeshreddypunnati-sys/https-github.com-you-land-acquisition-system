Government of India Enterprise Portal

End-to-End Digital Monitoring of Land Acquisition

A unified digital platform developed for the Department of Land Resources (DoLR) to ensure complete statutory compliance under the RFCTLARR Act, 2013.

  • Statutory Workflow Tracking: Proposal → Sec 4 SIA → Sec 11 Notification → Valuation → Sec 19 Award → PFMS Compensation → R&R.
  • Inter-Department Interoperability: Automated synchronization with State Bhoomi, IGRS Deeds, Bhuvan GIS, and NHAI.
  • AI Delay Prediction: Decision support system alerting collectors on compensation and litigation bottlenecks.
  • Data Protection & Privacy: Strict PII masking (Aadhaar, Bank Accounts, Landowner Names) under DPDP Act 2023.
⚖️ LEGAL NOTICE: Unauthorized access to this Government of India system is prohibited and punishable under Section 43 & 66 of the Information Technology Act, 2000. All activities are cryptographically audit-logged.

      About NLAMS Portal

      The National Land Acquisition & Management System (NLAMS) is an initiative of the Department of Land Resources, Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India. It provides a single-window transparent mechanism for statutory land acquisition tracking under RFCTLARR Act, 2013.
